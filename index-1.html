About NEMTECH

NEMTECH stands for Noble Emmanuel's Graphics & Tech. The brand was founded on September 29, 2019 and officially launched on January 26, 2026. Its focus is bringing brands back to life through powerful visuals and digital solutions.

Founder & CEO

Prod. Agbia Ugochukwu Emmanuel

Also known as Noble Emmanuel and Agonuel, he is a young Nigerian graphics designer, animator, technology enthusiast, creative, entrepreneur and visionary.

He began his graphics journey in 2019 through self-inspired learning and has interests in graphics design, animation and digital creativity. He also has leadership experience as Head Boy of Peter Marist College, Ifitedunu, and as Assistant Secretary of CYON at St. Mary's Catholic Church, Ifitedunu.
